# Visual System

## Design Intent

Create a modern Chinese ink-wash interface: calm, refined, tactile, and readable. The site should feel like a contemporary product shaped by xuan paper, brush rhythm, seal-red accents, and restrained classical atmosphere.

## Palette

Use these as starting tokens, adapting to the target brand when needed:

- `--ink-bg: #f6f1e6`
- `--ink-paper: #fffaf0`
- `--ink-paper-deep: #eee3d0`
- `--ink-text: #201c18`
- `--ink-muted: #6f675d`
- `--ink-line: #2a2723`
- `--ink-wash: rgba(32, 28, 24, 0.08)`
- `--ink-cinnabar: #b64232`
- `--ink-indigo: #2f536f`
- `--ink-jade: #5c7f67`
- `--ink-ochre: #b78642`
- `--ink-gold: #c8a65a`

Keep contrast strong. Cinnabar and gold are accents, not large-field background colors.

## Surfaces

- Use warm rice-paper backgrounds rather than pure white.
- Add paper tactility through subtle noise, layered gradients, or a very light repeated texture only when it does not harm readability.
- Use hand-inked borders sparingly: uneven 1px-2px lines, brush corner marks, or soft outline overlays.
- Prefer shallow layered paper panels over glassmorphism or neon glow.

## Typography

- Keep body text in readable system or high-quality sans/serif fonts.
- Use calligraphy-inspired display faces only for large headings or seals, and only if the project already includes a suitable font.
- Avoid applying brush fonts to dense UI labels, buttons, forms, or tables.

## Layout

- Use generous white space, but preserve the site's workflow density.
- Let icons and brush marks support hierarchy: feature cards, section labels, empty states, onboarding, and soft dividers.
- For dashboards, use compact tables and filters with ink tokens, not oversized editorial hero sections.

## Motifs

Good motifs:

- Dry-brush divider lines.
- Cinnabar seal chips for status, selected state, or highlights.
- Pale ink circles behind hero objects.
- Ink icons as semantic visual anchors.
- Jade or indigo accents for links, focus, and secondary actions.

Avoid:

- Heavy red/gold antique palace style.
- Fantasy game UI, ornate frames, lantern overload, dragons as generic decoration.
- Dirty paper that makes text look aged or low contrast.
- Replacing usable controls with decorative objects.
